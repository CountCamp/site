What you may do with it

Everything on this site is free: the book, the exercise books in R, JASP and SPSS, the toys and the tables, the archive. Read it, download it, print it, work through it — that is what it is here for.

NL·EN

For yourself: go ahead

Whether you are a student, a teacher, a psychologist, a researcher, or you simply want to teach yourself statistics — use what you need. You don’t have to ask, and you don’t have to give anything back.

Linking is always fine

Want to send someone here — a colleague, a friend, your class? Share the link. Put it in an email, a forum post, your course guide or your syllabus. Pointing people here is never something to ask permission for; it is exactly what I hope you will do.

Degree programmes: please get in touch first

If a programme wants to build the material into its teaching — as required reading, in its virtual learning environment, in a course reader, as course material — I would like to hear about it beforehand. Mail me at vragen@countcamp.org, and we will agree on something that works for both of us.

The difference is between linking to and building in: anyone may link to the site, always; making the material itself part of a curriculum starts with a conversation. That goes for the book and for the exercise books alike.

Support

I build CountCamp in the hours around my paid work. I would much rather work on it full-time — more room simply means more book: chapters that aren’t there yet, exercise books that could be better. If you would like to help that along, you can do so here.
